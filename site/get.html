Get Q-notes for Chrome

One download, about a minute to set up. Then highlight any lecture PDF, slide deck or web page and watch your notes build themselves.

Download Q-notes (.zip)

Always the newest version. Free, no sign-up to download.

Add it to Chrome (one time)

  1. Unzip the file you downloaded (right-click › Extract All). Keep the qnotes folder somewhere it won't be deleted, like Documents.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions (paste it in the address bar).
  3. Switch on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the qnotes folder. A short tour opens.
  5. Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar and pin Q-notes.

Then

  1. Make your account: open Q-notes › ⚙ Settings › Account & sync. Email or Google. The AI comes with it.
  2. Open a lecture: Home › Open a document, pick a PDF, slides or Word file, and start highlighting.

On your phone

Open lazy-notes-c07b.onrender.com and log in with the same account to read your notes, open your documents and ask the AI.

Updating

Q-notes tells you when there's a newer version. Download it, copy the new files over the old ones in your qnotes folder, and press Reload Q-notes in the notice. Your notes stay.
